PHOTO CULL: INSTALLING

1. Double-click "Install Photo Cull".

2. The first time, macOS says it can't check it, because it isn't from the App Store. That's expected:
     - click "Done" (not "Move to Bin"),
     - open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     - scroll down to the message about "Install Photo Cull" and click "Open Anyway",
     - confirm with Touch ID or your password.

3. Click "Install". It takes a few minutes the first time. When it says it's done, click "Open Photo Cull".

From then on, open Photo Cull from Applications or Launchpad like any app. When there's a new version,
Photo Cull shows an Update button: one click and it updates itself.
