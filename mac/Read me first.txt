PHOTO CULL: INSTALLING

1. Double-click "Install Photo Cull.command".

2. The first time, macOS will probably say it can't check the file is safe, because it isn't from
   the App Store. That's expected. Click "Done" (not "Move to Bin"), then:
     - open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     - scroll down to the message about "Install Photo Cull.command" and click "Open Anyway",
     - confirm with your password or Touch ID if asked.
   (Or: open Terminal, type  bash  and a space, drag the installer file onto the Terminal window, press Return.)

3. A Terminal window shows the progress. The first install downloads about 250 MB and takes a few
   minutes. When it says "Done!", Photo Cull opens in your web browser.

From then on, open "Photo Cull" from Applications, Launchpad or Spotlight like any other app.

To remove it, double-click "Uninstall Photo Cull.command" (your photos are never touched).
